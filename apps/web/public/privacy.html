Privacy

What you read stays on your device. This page lists every request Potato Browser and this site make to us, what each one carries, and what we keep.

Applies to Potato Browser for Mac, Potato Browser for iPhone and iPad, and potatobrowser.com. Last updated November 9, 2026.

In short

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared.
  • History, bookmarks, passwords, settings and downloads live on your device. Passwords are encrypted with a key in your Keychain.
  • The flag service learns hash prefixes that thousands of posts share, never the posts or pages you read.
  • The daily check says which lists, which app and which version. We count it, nothing more.
  • Crash reports are off unless you switch them on. Site reports are sent only when you press Report.
  • We keep no IP addresses. Cloudflare, which hosts this site, sees them to deliver each request.

On your device

Browsing history, bookmarks, saved passwords, site permissions, downloads, settings, the posts you flagged and the daily counts on the new tab page are stored in your profile on your Mac, iPhone or iPad. They are never uploaded, and there is no sync. Passwords are encrypted with a key kept in the macOS Keychain or the iOS Keychain.

The AI Shield, the ad blocker and the flag buttons work inside the browser. Which pages you visit, what is on them and what got hidden or blocked are never sent anywhere.

Private windows (Mac) and private tabs (iPhone and iPad) keep nothing once they close, and vote under a new random id each time.

That counter is how we know how many installs are in use.

Update check (Mac) Every 4 hours The Mac's architecture (Apple silicon or Intel). The random id the updater would add is removed before the request leaves. Nothing. The new version downloads from GitHub, see below.
Site report Only when you press Report in the AI Shield or ad blocker panel The page address, including what follows ? such as search words but without the part after #, what is wrong (AI still showing, ads still showing, page broken), the app and its version All of it, for fixing the rules. Deleted 30 days after we looked at it, and after 90 days at the latest.
Crash report Only if you switched crash reports on (welcome page or Settings), after a crash Mac: a minidump of the crashed process: the app, Electron and macOS versions, the architecture, the process type and a snapshot of its memory at that moment, which can contain parts of pages you had open. iPhone and iPad: the crash or hang diagnostics iOS makes: the version, the device model, the iOS version and where in the code it happened, with the region setting and process number removed. The report, stored with Cloudflare R2 and read only to fix the bug. Deleted after 90 days. The random install id the Mac crash reporter puts in the form and in the report is removed, and your IP address is not kept.
Download links When you download the Mac app from this site The architecture you picked A counter per day and architecture.

Every request goes over HTTPS without cookies. Limits against abuse count requests per IP address in memory for a minute and are never written down. The service logs errors only, never requests.

Other services the browser talks to

Websites you visit see what they see in any browser. Potato Browser sends Global Privacy Control, asking them not to sell or share your data, unless you switch it off.

Your search engine gets your searches, and, when search suggestions are on, what you type into the address bar. You pick it: DuckDuckGo, Google, Bing, Brave, Startpage, Ecosia or Kagi.

GitHub hosts the downloads and the Mac app's updates, so GitHub sees your IP address when you download them, as with any download.

Apple distributes the iPhone and iPad app through the App Store and TestFlight. As in Safari, WebKit's fraudulent website warning is on there and checks addresses against Safe Browsing lists the way Safari does. If you share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may pass us crash logs, as it does for any app.

The Mac app is built on Electron, which has none of Chrome's Google services: no sign-in, no sync, no usage statistics and no Safe Browsing lookups.

This website

No cookies. A self-hosted copy of Plausible counts visits without personal data: the page, the referring site, the browser, the operating system, the device type and the country, which it works out from your IP address without storing the address.

The site runs on Cloudflare, which processes each request (and so your IP address) to deliver it, under its own privacy policy. The flag counts and counters above are stored in Cloudflare D1, crash reports in Cloudflare R2.

Your choices

  • Turn off filter list updates (Settings, Ad blocker) and AI Shield rule updates (Settings, AI Shield): the daily check stops.
  • Turn flags off per platform in Settings, AI flags. Leaving flagged posts alone and hiding the buttons stops the lookups too.
  • Crash reports stay off unless you switch them on; switching them off again also drops reports not sent yet on the Mac.
  • Take a vote back any time: press the button on the post again.
  • Everything on your device goes when you delete the app and its data.

Questions and changes

Potato Browser is open source, so all of the above can be checked in its code. Ask anything in a GitHub issue, or report a security problem privately through GitHub's security advisories.

When this page changes, the change shows in the repository's history, and the date at the top moves.
