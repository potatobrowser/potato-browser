Grow your own

git clone https://github.com/potatobrowser/potato-browser
cd potato-browser
pnpm install
pnpm --filter @potato/browser dist --arch host

Needs macOS 13, Node.js 22 and pnpm 12. The DMG lands in apps/browser/release.

Questions

Why a potato?

Potatoes are about as un-artificial as it gets. They grow in dirt and have never summarized a meeting.

Is it free?

Yes, and open source under the MIT license. No account, no subscription, no ads.

Can I get the AI back on a site?

Click the shield in the toolbar. It comes back on that site only. Settings lists every covered feature if you want to keep a few.

Why block ads in a no-AI browser?

They grow in the same patch. Much of the generated filler online exists to show ads, and ad networks are the trackers that follow you around.

Will the ad blocker break sites?

Rarely. uBlock Origin's lists carry fixes and update daily. If a site acts up, switch the ad blocker off for it from the toolbar.

What does the ad blocker send?

Nothing about you. Matching happens on your Mac. Once a day the browser asks this site which filter lists changed; the request names the lists, never you or your pages. You can turn updates off in Settings.

How are flags anonymous?

No account, no email. To check a page, the browser sends only the first five characters of each post's fingerprint, which thousands of posts share. Post ids, links and page contents never leave your Mac. Your flags are stored under a scrambled id, and private windows use a new one each time.

Can't people just flag everything?

Not easily. Each install counts once per post, and so does each network, with a daily limit per install. You choose how many flags it takes, and a buried post is one click away.

What does the flag service store?

Per post: a one-way fingerprint of its id, the platform and a count. Per flag: a scrambled version of your browser's random id (so you can undo it), the hour (for the daily limit) and a network bucket from 0 to 65,535 derived from the first part of your IP address. Hundreds of networks share each bucket, so it identifies no one. No IP addresses, no URLs, no accounts.

Why do downloads come from GitHub?

So you can check them. GitHub Actions builds each release from the public source and keeps the build log and a signed record of the commit. Run gh attestation verify on the file to confirm. This site only counts downloads per day.

Is the app signed?

Yes, with an Apple Developer ID, and notarized by Apple. spctl --assess -vv "/Applications/Potato Browser.app" should say Notarized Developer ID.

Does it save passwords?

Yes. It offers to keep a password when you sign in and fills it when you pick it from its own list. Passwords are encrypted with a key in your macOS Keychain and never leave your Mac. Bring yours over from Chrome, Safari, Firefox or 1Password with a CSV export.

Extensions?

Not yet, they're still sprouting. The ad blocker and the password manager are built in.

Windows or Linux?

Mac only for now. Other soils, maybe later.
