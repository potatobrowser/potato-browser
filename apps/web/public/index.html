What it hides

The AI Shield knows the AI features of the sites people use most and hides them before the page finishes loading. The articles, results and messages stay where they are. If you want the AI back on one site, the shield button in the toolbar turns it on for that site only.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. No account, no subscription, no ads.

Can I see the AI on a site when I actually want it?

Yes. Click the shield in the toolbar and switch it off for that site. Everything comes back right away, on that site only. Settings has the full list of covered sites and features if you want to keep some of them.

Can't people just flag everything?

Each install counts once per post, there is a daily limit per install, and the service rate-limits by network. You choose how many flags it takes before a post counts as AI-generated, and a hidden post is always one click away.

What exactly does the flag service store?

For each flagged post: a one-way fingerprint of the post's id, the platform name, and a count. For each flag: a scrambled version of your browser's random id, so you can undo it. No IP addresses, no URLs, no accounts.

Why does macOS warn me the first time?

Apple asks developers to send apps through its notarization service. This first release isn't notarized yet, so macOS asks you to confirm once in System Settings. Notarized builds are planned.

Does it support extensions and saved passwords?

Not yet. Your password manager's own app works alongside it in the meantime.

Windows or Linux?

Mac only for now.
