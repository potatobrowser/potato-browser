Potato Browser is a web browser built on WKWebView. It needs no account and has no in-app purchases.

Background audio (UIBackgroundModes: audio): web pages keep playing audio and video when the app goes to the background or the screen locks, as in Safari, for example a podcast, a radio stream or a YouTube video. The app plays nothing of its own.

To see the main features:
1. Search for "how do magnets work" on google.com: the AI Overview is hidden. Tap the shield in the address bar to see what was hidden, and switch it off for the site to bring it back.
2. Open reddit.com or m.youtube.com: posts get an AI? button for flagging AI-generated content anonymously, and a Not AI button when a post others flagged was made by a person.
3. Open a news site such as theguardian.com: ads and trackers are blocked; the shield panel shows how many.

Network: the app talks to potatobrowser.com only for anonymous flag lookups and votes, a daily filter list and rule check, reports the user sends, and crash reports when the user opts in. Details: https://potatobrowser.com/privacy

UIFileSharingEnabled: downloaded files are kept in the app's Documents folder so they show in the Files app.

We have requested the default browser entitlement (com.apple.developer.web-browser) separately; this build does not use it.
